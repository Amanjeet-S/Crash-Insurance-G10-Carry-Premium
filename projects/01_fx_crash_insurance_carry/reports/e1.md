# E1 and E4: estimation record

Stage 3 of the [research design](../research_design.md): construction, samples, inference and aggregate results. Month-level series stay in `data/private/results/` under the licence terms.

## Reproduction

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/calibrate_smiles.py --start 2010-07-30 --end 2026-08-31
.venv/bin/python scripts/calibrate_smiles.py --contributor FN --start 2007-01-31 --end 2026-08-31
.venv/bin/python scripts/estimate_e1.py
```

## Construction

- **Legs.** At each New York month-end, the available currencies are ranked by forward discount fd = log(X/F_X), with X in USD per unit of the currency. The portfolio is long the three highest and short the three lowest; the extended sample is two and two.
- **Exclusion rule.** A currency without the calibration set (spot, forward, rate, ATM, 25Δ risk reversal, 25Δ butterfly) is excluded that month. A month is used only if at least twice the number of legs remain.
- **Protective options.** Each leg is protected by a one-month option that pays when the leg loses: a put on the currency, which is a USD call for USD-base pairs. The strike is the 10Δ strike on the calibrated smile, in the pair's delta convention. `src/qef/fx/crash.py` gives the mapping.
- **Units.** V_smile and V_flat are the premia at the smile volatility and at the ATM volatility, in USD per USD of forward notional at delivery. For both quotation types this equals the undiscounted premium on the quoted pair divided by the pair forward.
- **Estimand.** C_skew = (1/n) Σ (V_smile − V_flat), FD = (1/n)(Σ_long fd − Σ_short fd) and φ = C_skew / FD.
- **Readings and comparisons.** E1 uses the market-strangle reading and E4 the smile-strangle reading. 25Δ is a comparison. The ATM comparison is identically zero, because the calibrated smile passes through the ATM volatility.
- **Timing.** Spot, delivery and expiry are built on the New York business calendar (`src/qef/data/smile_inputs.py`). Volatility time is (expiry − trade)/365.

## Samples

- **Primary.** Composite quotes, 3L/3S, from the primary start set by the audit rule to the last complete month.
- **Extended.** Fenics quotes before the primary start, 2L/2S.
- **Regimes.** Zero-rate: primary start to December 2021. Hiking: January 2022 onwards.

## Inference (pre-registered)

- Regime means with Newey and West (1987) standard errors. The bandwidth follows the automatic procedure of Newey and West (1994) without prewhitening.
- The difference is estimated as the coefficient on a regime dummy, with a Newey–West standard error.
- A stationary-bootstrap percentile interval (Politis and Romano, 1994), resampling each regime separately with its own automatic block length (Politis and White, 2004; Patton, Politis and White, 2009), with 9,999 draws.

## Supplementary analyses (added after the first results)

Recorded in the research log on 24 September 2026. They do not replace the pre-registered estimand.

- The standard error of the difference across Newey–West lags from 0 to 20.
- An AR(1) plug-in standard error, and the effective sample size per regime, T(1 − ρ)/(1 + ρ).
- Medians, 10% trimmed means and the log-φ contrast.
- The ratio of regime means, mean C_skew / mean FD.
- Separate regime differences for C_skew and FD.
- The difference excluding 2020–2021, and excluding the largest zero-rate observations.

## Splice test

The skew cost of the long-side and short-side protective options is compared leg by leg between Fenics and composite quotes. The comparison covers every currency-month where both have the calibration set, reported separately before and within the primary sample.

**Interpretation under the pre-registered rules.**

- The regime difference in φ is not claimed: both intervals include zero, under both butterfly readings and both hedge deltas.
- The skew price of crash insurance per month is essentially the same in the two regimes.
- The forward-discount spread of the carry portfolio is significantly higher in the hiking regime.
- The butterfly reading changes φ by about 4 per cent (E4 against E1).

**Supplementary analyses (post hoc).**

- φ is highly persistent (first-order autocorrelation 0.85 and 0.76), leaving roughly 8 effective observations per regime. An AR(1) plug-in standard error of the difference is 0.27 (t = −1.31).
- Excluding 2020–2021, the zero-rate months with the smallest forward discounts, the difference is −0.03. The median of φ falls from 0.53 to 0.38.

**Splice test.** Fenics and composite skew costs agree leg by leg (correlation 0.995 before the primary sample and 0.997 within it).

**Extended sample.** Fenics quotes, February 2007 to April 2013, 72 month-ends. The mean C_skew is 22.3 bp and the mean φ is 0.64. Fenics butterflies before 2010 are often stale, so this sample is secondary evidence.
